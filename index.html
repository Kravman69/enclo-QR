Hormone Considerations

Enclomiphene can influence hormone levels by acting on the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis. As with any research compound that affects hormones, regular bloodwork is recommended before, during, and after use to monitor relevant markers and better understand individual response.

It works by reducing estrogen-mediated negative feedback, which can lead to increased LH and FSH in many research contexts. Individual responses vary, and monitoring helps ensure the research goals are being met safely.
